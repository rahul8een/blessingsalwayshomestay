About us

Blessings Always is a neat, clean, and spiritually uplifting retreat on Old Mussoorie Road. What sets it apart is the rare combination of comfort, serenity, and versatility:
* A huge terrace with breathtaking mountain views, perfect for relaxation, yoga sessions, or evening gatherings.
* A spiritual garden that creates a calm, positive atmosphere—ideal for meditation, reflection, or simply unwinding.
* Fresh, clean rooms (including balcony rooms with views) designed for groups, families, and corporates.
* A peaceful, wholesome environment that makes guests feel rejuvenated.
* The property’s large spaces (garden + terrace) make it especially suited for group stays, corporate retreats, intimate weddings, and even film shoots.

Got Questions?

Where is Blessings Always Homestay located?

We are located on Old Mussoorie Road, Opposite Uttranchal Ayurvedic College, Dehradun, Uttarakhand  — just a short drive from key attractions, viewpoints, and the road leading to Mussoorie.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes, we offer complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi in all rooms and common areas.

Are meals available at the homestay?

We provide homely, freshly prepared meals on request. Tea and snacks are also available throughout the day.

Do you offer parking?

Yes, we have free parking available for all guests.

What is the check-in and check-out time?

Check-in: 1:00 PM
Check-out: 11:00 AM
Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability.

Are pets allowed?

At the moment, we do not allow pets to ensure comfort and hygiene for all guests.
